Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — to display your expenses, generate summaries, and support filtering and reporting features. We do not use your personal financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app. We may use aggregated, anonymised usage statistics to improve the product.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. While we strive to protect your data, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted third-party services for hosting and infrastructure. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may request a copy of your data or ask us to remove your account and all associated information by contacting us. We will respond to such requests within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website. We take privacy seriously and will do our best to address your concerns promptly.
